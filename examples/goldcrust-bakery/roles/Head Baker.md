## Purpose

### Driver

The [[Bakehouse]] needs one person accountable for what gets baked and to what standard,
so quality and the morning schedule don't drift.

### Requirement

A single role that owns the recipe range, the daily bake plan, and the food-safety
standard.

## Description

The Head Baker curates the standing range, decides recipes and the daily bake plan, sets
the bake schedule, and holds the quality bar for everything that leaves the [[Bakehouse]].

## Concerns

Quality consistency, food-safety compliance, and protecting the morning dispatch window.

## Accountabilities

- Own the recipe range and the daily bake plan.
- Sign off quality before dispatch.
- Train and supervise the bake team.

## Decision Rights

- Decides recipes and the daily bake sequence (autonomous).
- Curates the standing range and proposes changes to it — a range change is a
  [[Bakery Strategy]] bet under the [[Daily Range Cap]]; [[Mara Holt]] holds the final
  call. (Today both hats sit on Mara — the split still matters: it survives a hand-over
  of the role.)
- Consulted on hiring for the [[Bakehouse]]; the owner decides.

## Evaluation

### Metrics and Monitoring

- Quality complaints per week; dispatch on-time rate; unsold-waste ratio.
