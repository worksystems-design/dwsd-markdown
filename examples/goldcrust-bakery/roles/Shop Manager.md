## Purpose

### Driver

Each shop needs someone accountable for daily trading and for feeding accurate demand back
to production.

### Requirement

A role that owns opening and closing, sell-through, and the next-day replenishment order.

## Description

The Shop Manager runs a single shop location end to end: staffing the counter, selling the
range, counting closing stock, and placing the [[Daily Replenishment Order]].

## Concerns

Morning availability, customer experience at peak, and demand-signal accuracy.

## Accountabilities

- Open and close the shop; staff the counter.
- Place an accurate next-day [[Daily Replenishment Order]].
- Report stockouts and demand patterns to [[Bakery Operations]].

## Decision Rights

- Decides daily counter staffing and replenishment quantities (autonomous).
- Escalates range changes to the [[Head Baker]].

## Evaluation

### Metrics and Monitoring

- Morning stockout incidents; sell-through rate; replenishment accuracy.
