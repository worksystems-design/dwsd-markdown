## Purpose

### Driver

With two shops, a delivery run and a growing wholesale book, [[Bakery Operations]] needs
one person who holds the day-to-day together — so [[Mara Holt]] can stay in the
[[Bakehouse]] in the mornings instead of on the phone.

### Requirement

A single role that owns daily coordination across the shops, [[Deliveries]] and
[[Wholesale]], and chairs the [[Daily Production Sync]] when the owner is baking.

## Description

The Head of Operations runs [[Bakery Operations]] end to end: the shop managers, the
delivery team and the wholesale account report through this role. It keeps the
[[Daily Replenishment Flow]] moving, sequences wholesale enquiries under the
[[Wholesale Capacity Cap]], and is the first escalation point when a dispatch slips.

Held by [[Jonas Keller]] since September 2026 — see
[[Jonas Keller Leads Operations]] for the decision that created the role.

## Concerns

Morning dispatch punctuality, the balance between shop replenishment and wholesale volume,
and keeping the coordination load off the [[Head Baker]].

## Accountabilities

- Run the [[Daily Production Sync]] when the [[Head Baker]] is baking.
- Keep the two shops, [[Deliveries]] and [[Wholesale]] coordinated day to day.
- Hold the [[Wholesale Capacity Cap]] and sequence enquiries at the limit.

## Decision Rights

- Decides daily trade-offs between shop replenishment and wholesale drops (autonomous).
- Decides delivery routing and dispatch order (autonomous).
- Consulted on range and pricing; [[Mara Holt]] decides.

## Evaluation

### Metrics and Monitoring

- Morning dispatch on-time rate; stockout incidents across both shops; wholesale book
  against the cap.
