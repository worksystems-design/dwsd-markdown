Market Street is a **site** — the older of the two shops, in the town center. Its people are members of this unit and of the
[[Market Street Shop]] work system: the site says *where* they work, the work system says which flow
they work in.

> **Modeling note.** The two are drawn in different views, the site in the unit tree, the
