Deliveries is the two-person team ([[Pia Brandt]], [[Nils Vogt]]) that runs the morning
run from the [[Bakehouse]] to the two shops. It is a **plain team**, not a work system: its
`unit-type` is just `team`, with **no `flightlevel`** and **no `work-system`**.

It participates in the flow that [[Bakery Operations]] (FL2) coordinates — it carries the
morning dispatch — but the delivery itself isn't a domain the bakery models as its own work
system. Contrast it with the [[Bakehouse]], which *is* both a team **and** a work system
(`unit-type: [team, work-system]`).
