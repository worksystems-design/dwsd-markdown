Mill Lane is where the bread is made: the bakehouse, the small office upstairs and the
yard the delivery van leaves from at five. It is a **site** — a unit cut by *where people
work*, not by how the work flows. The [[Bakehouse]] work system, [[Bakery Operations]]
and the two plain teams [[Deliveries]] and [[Wholesale]] all have their people here.
