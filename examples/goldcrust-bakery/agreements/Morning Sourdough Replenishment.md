## Driver and Requirement

Answers [[Dependable Morning Sourdough]] via the consented [[Prioritize Morning Sourdough]]
proposal: the [[Market Street Shop]] must have sourdough available through the morning peak.

## Who is responsible for what?

- [[Bakehouse]] — bakes a guaranteed morning sourdough quota and dispatches it first.
- [[Market Street Shop]] — counts and reports sourdough sell-out time daily.
- [[Bakery Operations]] — reviews the quota weekly against demand.

## Description

A fixed minimum sourdough quota is reserved for the [[Market Street Shop]] in the first
dispatch each morning, ahead of other lines. The quota is reviewed weekly and adjusted to
demand.

## Evaluation Criteria

- No sourdough stockout before 11:00 at the [[Market Street Shop]].
- Reserved-quota waste stays within the normal unsold-waste ratio.

## Concerns

If the reserved quota is set too high it raises waste; the weekly review exists to keep
that in check.
