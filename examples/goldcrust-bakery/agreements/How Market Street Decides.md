## Driver and Requirement

[[Market Street]] may spend up to $1,000 a day on the site. A mandate held by a team
needs the team to say how it decides — otherwise everyone waits for everyone.

## Who is responsible for what?

- Every member of [[Market Street]] — today [[Ines Rossi]] and [[Lena Frei]] — may act on
  the site's spending mandate as described below.
- [[Bakery Operations]] — takes anything above the daily limit.

## Description

- **Up to $200:** any member decides alone, no need to ask.
- **Above $200:** two members decide together. On a site of two, that is both.
- **Above $1,000 in a day:** outside the site's mandate — it goes to [[Bakery Operations]].
  The daily limit counts everything the site spends that day, by all members together.

Whoever spends writes it in the site cash book the same day. Being
[[Market Street Shop Manager]] gives no extra say here: the site's money is the site's.

## Evaluation Criteria

- Small repairs happen the same day, without a phone call.
- The cash book matches the receipts at the end of the month.
