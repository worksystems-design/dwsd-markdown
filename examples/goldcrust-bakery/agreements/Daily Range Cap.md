## Driver and Requirement

Every product in the daily range costs prep time, ingredients, and waste risk inside a
fixed morning bake window. Without a limit, each [[New Product Introduction]] quietly
stretches the [[Bakehouse]] thinner. The range needs a WIP limit like any other flow.

## Who is responsible for what?

- [[Head Baker]] — curates the range and names the swap candidate when a new product
  comes in.
- [[Bakery Strategy]] — decides range bets only within the cap; [[Mara Holt]] holds the
  final range call.
- [[Bakery Operations]] — schedules the rollout and the phase-out it replaces.

## Description

The standing daily range holds **at most twelve products**. A [[New Product Introduction]]
enters only by replacing an existing product, or explicitly time-boxed as a seasonal
special. The check happens *before* a range bet is `Decided` on the
[[Bakery Strategy Board]] — which is exactly where this agreement anchors.

## Evaluation Criteria

- The range never exceeds twelve standing products.
- Waste rate and morning bake window hold steady as products rotate.
