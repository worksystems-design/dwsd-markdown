## Description

Set the opening hours posted on the door of [[Market Street]] and keep them current —
holidays, a late delivery, a closed street. The hours stay within the band the
[[Market Street Shop Manager]] decides under the [[Shop Manager Mandate]]: the shop says
how long it trades, the site lead says what the door says.

## Evaluation

- The posted hours match the hours the shop actually opens.

> [!NOTE]
> **Modeling note.** The mandate does not name who holds it. The
> [[Market Street Site Lead]] names it in its `## Mandates` table, as a meeting names an
> interaction; `for-domain` says whose mandate it is, `mandate-of` where it shows.
