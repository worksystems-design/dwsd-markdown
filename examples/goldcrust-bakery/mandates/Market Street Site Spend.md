## Description

[[Market Street]] may spend up to $1,000 a day on the site — repairs, supplies, a
locksmith — without asking. How the members decide among themselves is in
[[How Market Street Decides]]. Anything above the daily limit goes to
[[Bakery Operations]].

## Evaluation

- The site cash book shows every spend on the day it happened.
- No day goes above the limit without [[Bakery Operations]] having said yes.

> [!NOTE]
> **Modeling note.** A mandate pulled out of a row into its own file, because it has a
> band to stay in and a first review to keep. The limit is new, so the review comes after
> five weeks rather than six months; the agreement on how the site decides keeps its own,
> longer review.
