## Context

Until September 2026 every coordination question in [[Bakery Operations]] — a late van, a
shop short on sourdough, a café asking for a standing order — landed on [[Mara Holt]],
usually while she was baking. With a second shop, a delivery team and a wholesale book that
keeps growing, the owner had become the bottleneck of the day-to-day.

## Decision

Create the [[Head of Operations]] role and give it to [[Jonas Keller]]. The two shop
managers, [[Deliveries]] and [[Wholesale]] report to him; he chairs the
[[Daily Production Sync]] whenever Mara is in the [[Bakehouse]]. Mara keeps range, brand
and the final call on capacity.

## Consequences

- One reporting line for everything operational; Mara's mornings stay in the Bakehouse.
- Jonas becomes the single point of contact for the shops, the vans and the cafés — which
  is exactly the load that [[Head of Operations Is the Dispatch Bottleneck]] later reads
  as a risk.
- The role, not the person, carries the mandate: a hand-over does not reopen this decision.
