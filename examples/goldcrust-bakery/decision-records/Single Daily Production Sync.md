## Context

Production and the two shops had been coordinating in separate ad-hoc phone calls. Demand
information arrived late and inconsistently, and the [[Bakehouse]] often started baking
before the shops' numbers were in.

## Decision

Replace the per-shop calls with a single daily afternoon sync — the [[Daily Production Sync]] —
owned by [[Bakery Operations]], where both shops and the [[Bakehouse]] confirm the next-day
plan together.

## Consequences

- One shared demand picture; the bake plan is set against real numbers.
- A fixed 15-minute slot adds a small daily commitment for everyone.
- Makes [[Bakery Operations]] the clear home for cross-team coordination.
