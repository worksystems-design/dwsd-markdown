[[Bakery Operations]] needs someone who decides the day-to-day while the owner bakes.

## Current Situation

Shops, deliveries and wholesale all escalate to [[Mara Holt]], who is in the
[[Bakehouse]] most mornings.

## Effect

Decisions wait for hours; vans, shops and cafés work around each other instead of with
each other.

## Need

One person with a clear mandate for everything operational, reachable during the
morning.

## Consequences

Faster day-to-day decisions and mornings in the Bakehouse for Mara; the new owner of the
day-to-day becomes a single point of contact, which needs watching.
