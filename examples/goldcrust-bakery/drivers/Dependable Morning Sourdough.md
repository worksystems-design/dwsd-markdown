The [[Market Street Shop]] needs dependable sourdough availability through the morning peak.

## Current Situation

Sourdough sells out before 09:00 on most weekdays. The morning dispatch quantity is fixed
by habit and does not track recent demand.

## Effect

The shop misses its highest-margin morning window and disappoints regulars — observed in
the sell-out times, not just assumed.

## Need

The [[Market Street Shop]] team needs a reliable morning sourdough supply it can count on
before opening.

## Consequences

Capturing the morning peak protects margin and keeps regulars; it also makes demand more
predictable for the [[Bakehouse]].
