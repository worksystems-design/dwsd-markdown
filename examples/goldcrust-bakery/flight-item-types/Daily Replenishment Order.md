## Where do Flight Items of this type come from?

From each shop's evening stock count: the gap between closing stock and next-day expected
demand becomes a replenishment order for the [[Bakehouse]].

## Why are you working on these? What is the customer value?

They keep the shops stocked with fresh product for the next trading day — the core of the
bakery's daily value.

## To whom is the finished Flight Item delivered?

To the [[Market Street Shop]] and [[Station Road Shop]] in the morning dispatch.

## How long does it take?

One overnight cycle: ordered in the afternoon, baked overnight, dispatched before opening.
