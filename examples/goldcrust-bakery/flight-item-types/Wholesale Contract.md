## Where do Flight Items of this type come from?

From a café, restaurant, or hotel asking for a standing daily bread order. One item is one
wholesale account, from first enquiry to a stable recurring delivery.

## Why are you working on these? What is the customer value?

A dependable wholesale base load: predictable volume that the [[Bakehouse]] can plan around,
smoothing demand swings the retail shops cannot.

## To whom is the finished Flight Item delivered?

To the wholesale customer — a recurring early-morning delivery on agreed terms.

## How long does it take?

Days to a few weeks to set up (samples, terms, first delivery); then it runs as a standing
order until changed or ended.
