## Where do Flight Items of this type come from?

From a strategic bet at [[Bakery Strategy]] (FL3): a decision to open a new retail location.
One item is one location, from site discovery to first trading day.

## Why are you working on these? What is the customer value?

Growth — each new shop extends the bakery's reach to a new neighborhood, and is the main way
the bakery scales beyond its current footprint.

## To whom is the finished Flight Item delivered?

To a new local customer base, and to the bakery as a running retail unit (a new work system
handed over to [[Bakery Operations]]).

## How long does it take?

Months, not days: site search, lease, fit-out, hiring, and a soft opening before the launch.
