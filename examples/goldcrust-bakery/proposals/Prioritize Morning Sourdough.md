## Proposal

Reserve a fixed minimum sourdough quota for the [[Market Street Shop]] in the first morning
dispatch, ahead of other lines, and review the quota weekly against demand.

## Objections

None sustained. A concern that a fixed quota could raise waste was addressed by the weekly
review.

## Outcome

Consented on 2026-05-22. Implemented as the [[Morning Sourdough Replenishment]] agreement.
