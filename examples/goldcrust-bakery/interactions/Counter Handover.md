## Purpose

The morning hand-off where the [[Bakehouse]] dispatch is received and checked in at the
shop counter before opening. A short, recurring interaction — not a meeting with a canvas,
just a defined moment of contact between two work systems.

The dispatch arrives, the shop counts it against the [[Daily Replenishment Order]], and any
shortfall is flagged immediately to [[Bakery Operations]].
