## Purpose

Bake the daily range of breads and pastries to a dependable quality and schedule, so the
shops always have what they need to open.

## Key Responsibilities

- Produce the daily bread and pastry range to recipe and food-safety standard.
- Hit the morning dispatch window so the shops are stocked before opening.
- Plan production against next-day demand from the shops.

## Customers and Deliverables

- [[Market Street Shop]] and [[Station Road Shop]] — baked goods, dispatched each morning.
- Wholesale customers — standing daily bread orders.

## Dependencies

Depends on reliable flour and ingredient supply, and on the evening stock counts from the
shops to plan the next day's [[Daily Replenishment Order]].

## External Constraints

- Ingredient lead times and price volatility (especially flour).
- Food-safety and hygiene regulation.

## Key Challenges

- Morning peaks: several lines must be ready in the same narrow dispatch window.
- Demand swings (weather, weekends) are hard to forecast at the product level.

## Key Resources

- The bake team ([[Mara Holt]], [[Tom Reuben]]), the ovens and proofers, the recipe book.

## Delegator Responsibilities

- [[Mara Holt]] sets the range and quality bar and secures ingredient supply.

## Competencies, Qualities and Skills

- Craft baking, production scheduling under time pressure, food-safety discipline.

## Key Metrics and Monitoring

- Morning dispatch on-time rate; unsold-waste ratio; sourdough availability at the shops.
