## Purpose

Sell the daily range to walk-in customers at the Market Street location, and feed real
demand back to production.

## Key Responsibilities

- Open stocked and sell through the day's range.
- Count closing stock and place the next-day [[Daily Replenishment Order]].
- Surface what sells out and when (see [[Sourdough Sold Out Before 9am]]).

## Customers and Deliverables

- Walk-in customers — fresh bread and pastries.
- [[Bakery Operations]] — accurate daily demand signals.

## Dependencies

Depends on the morning dispatch from the [[Bakehouse]] arriving before opening.

## Key Metrics and Monitoring

- Sell-through rate; morning stockouts; customer wait time at peak.
