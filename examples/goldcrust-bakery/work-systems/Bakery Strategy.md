## Purpose

Set the direction of the bakery — range, locations, and growth — and connect that strategy
to day-to-day operations. As FL3 this is the top of the chain; nothing contributes upward
from here.

## Key Responsibilities

- Decide the product range and which locations to run.
- Set quality and brand standards.
- Fund and prioritize improvements across the work systems.

## Customers and Deliverables

- [[Bakery Operations]] — clear priorities and constraints to coordinate within.

## Dependencies

Depends on operational signals (sales, waste, demand patterns) flowing up from
[[Bakery Operations]].

## Key Metrics and Monitoring

- Revenue and margin per location; range performance; customer return rate.
