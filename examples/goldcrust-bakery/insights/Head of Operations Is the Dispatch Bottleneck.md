Since [[Jonas Keller Leads Operations]], the morning dispatch and the wholesale phone both
run through one person at the same hour — the [[Head of Operations]] has become the
bottleneck of the dispatch window, not because of the volume but because of the timing.

**Category:** structure | **Severity:** warning

## Observations

- Wholesale calls cluster between 06:00 and 07:00, exactly the loading window (see
  [[Dispatch Mornings Double-Booked]]).
- The open Delivery Coordinator seat ([[NA1]]) is the role that would own the loading.
- The insight observes the **person**, not [[Bakery Operations]]: the domain works, the
  seat is overloaded.

## Suggested Actions

- Fill the Delivery Coordinator seat before the third van arrives.
- Move wholesale calls to the afternoon, after the [[Daily Production Sync]].
