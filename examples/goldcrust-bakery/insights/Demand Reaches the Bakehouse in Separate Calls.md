plan is set against whichever numbers happen to be in.

**Category:** flow | **Severity:** warning

## Observations

- Two shops, two calls, no fixed time (see [[Bake Plan Set Before the Shops Called]]).
- Nobody sees both shops' numbers side by side before production starts.

## Suggested Actions

- Bring both shops and the Bakehouse together once a day, before the bake plan is set.
