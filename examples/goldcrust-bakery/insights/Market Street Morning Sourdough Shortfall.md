Morning sourdough demand at the [[Market Street Shop]] consistently outstrips the quantity
dispatched, so the shop loses its highest-margin morning sales.

**Category:** flow | **Severity:** warning

## Observations

- Sells out before 09:00 on most weekdays (see [[Sourdough Sold Out Before 9am]]).
- The morning dispatch quantity is set by habit, not by recent demand.

## Suggested Actions

- Reserve a morning sourdough quota for the shop in the first dispatch.
