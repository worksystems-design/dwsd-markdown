## Where do Flight Items of this type come from?

From the [[Portfolio]]: a strategic bet (a new product line, a new market), a major fleet
contract [[Sales]] brings in, or a regulatory change that touches every charger. One epic
is one business outcome large enough to span several PIs of the [[Charger ART]].

## Why are you working on these? What is the customer value?

Epics are how Voltfeld decides what to become — which chargers, for whom, with what
footprint in the plant. Everything smaller is a consequence of one of them.

## To whom is the finished Flight Item delivered?

To the market, as a product in the field — and to the [[Portfolio]] itself, as the business
outcome it funded: revenue in a segment, a certification that opens a country, a cost
position.

## How long does it take?

Two to four PIs — half a year to a year. Longer when tooling or certification sit on the
critical path.
