## Where do Flight Items of this type come from?

From customers: a fleet operator or depot orders DC chargers through [[Sales]], an
installer orders wallboxes through the portal. One item is one order, entered in the ERP.

## Why are you working on these? What is the customer value?

A charger delivered on the confirmed date, configured as ordered and invoiced correctly.
Nothing about an order is interesting to the customer — until it goes wrong.

## To whom is the finished Flight Item delivered?

To the customer — chargers on site and an invoice — and, for the orders that needed the
studio, a reason code back into the [[Order Exceptions Review]].

## How long does it take?

Two to six weeks from order to delivery, set by the plant's schedule. A studio case should
add no more than two working days.
