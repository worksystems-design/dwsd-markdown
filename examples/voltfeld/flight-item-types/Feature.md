## Where do Flight Items of this type come from?

From [[Product Management]] splitting an [[Epic]] into pieces one PI can hold, and from
market requests that arrive feature-sized — a payment terminal variant, a new OCPP
profile, a quieter fan curve. One feature is one capability a customer can see, spanning
as many teams as it needs: a hardware change in [[HW Base]], firmware in the product line,
a backend change in [[Connectivity]].

## Why are you working on these? What is the customer value?

A feature is the unit the [[Charger ART]] plans, commits and demonstrates. It is the size at
which hardware, firmware and backend can be promised together and shown together.

## To whom is the finished Flight Item delivered?

To the customer as part of a system release — built by [[Manufacturing]] into new units,
and rolled out to the fleet over the air by [[Connectivity]] where firmware alone carries
it.

## How long does it take?

One PI — ten weeks — from PI planning to the system release. A feature that needs new
tooling or a certification slot usually takes two.
