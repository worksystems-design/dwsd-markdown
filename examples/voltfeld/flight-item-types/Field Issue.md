## Where do Flight Items of this type come from?

From the field: a charger fails on site, an installer calls, a fleet operator reports a
pattern, or the telemetry [[Connectivity]] collects flags one before anyone calls.
[[Field Service]] sees them first. One item is one observed problem — not yet a cause.

## Why are you working on these? What is the customer value?

A charger that does not charge is the only thing a customer notices. Field issues are the
ART's shortest feedback loop to the product in use — and the one flow that cannot wait
for the next PI when safety or a whole fleet is affected.

## To whom is the finished Flight Item delivered?

To the operator whose chargers are affected — as a hotfix over the air, a repair, or a
revised part — and back to [[Field Service]], who reported it and closes it with the
customer.

## How long does it take?

Days for a hotfix in the expedite lane; a PI when the fix is a design change and travels as
a [[Feature]].
