## Purpose

### Driver

The [[Wallbox]] team needs one person who turns the ART's features into a team backlog and
answers the team's questions daily — not a committee at FL2.

### Requirement

A product owner for the Wallbox product line: owns the team backlog, accepts stories, speaks for
installers and home owners.

## Description

The Product Owner of the [[Wallbox]] team splits the features [[Product Management]] ranks
into stories, orders the team backlog, and accepts the result. Kept by [[Lukas Weber]].

## Concerns

Installer feedback; the enclosure variants the channel asks for versus the platform the
team builds on.

## Accountabilities

- Own the Wallbox product-line backlog; accept stories.
- Represent the team's content at PI planning and the system demo.

## Decision Rights

- Orders the team backlog (autonomous).
- Scope of a feature — **agrees** with [[Product Management]].

## Evaluation

### Metrics and Monitoring

- Stories accepted per iteration; features delivered as planned at PI planning.
