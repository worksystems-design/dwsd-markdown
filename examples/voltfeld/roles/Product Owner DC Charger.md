## Purpose

### Driver

The [[DC Charger]] team sells to fleets and depots with contracts and commissioning dates;
it needs a product owner who knows those customers and can say no on their behalf.

### Requirement

A product owner for the DC Charger team: owns the team backlog, accepts stories, speaks for
fleet operators and site owners.

## Description

The Product Owner of the [[DC Charger]] team splits features into stories, orders the team
backlog, and accepts the result. Kept by [[Sofia Marques]]. She is the team's link to
[[Sales]], whose fleet deals arrive with dates attached.

## Concerns

Commitments made by [[Sales]] before the team has planned them; the DC rig queue at
[[System Integration]].

## Accountabilities

- Own the DC Charger team backlog; accept stories.
- Represent the team's content at PI planning and the system demo.

## Decision Rights

- Orders the team backlog (autonomous).
- Scope of a feature — **agrees** with [[Product Management]].

## Evaluation

### Metrics and Monitoring

- Stories accepted per iteration; customer commitments met per PI.
