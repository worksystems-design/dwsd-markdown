## Purpose

### Driver

An ART that plans ten weeks of work for six teams needs the people who carry the business
outcome in the room — to set priorities, assign business value, and accept the result.

### Requirement

A small group accountable for the business outcome of the [[Charger ART]], present at PI
planning and the system demo.

## Description

Business Owner is a **shared role**: four keepers, listed above. Together they represent the
[[Portfolio]] to the ART — [[Henrik Dahl]] for the company, [[Ingrid Halvorsen]] for
producibility and the plant, [[Niamh Byrne]] for the product, [[Rolf Steinbach]] for the
market and the field. Borrowed from SAFe. Anything scoped to this role with `for-domain`
lands on all four of them in the org chart.

## Concerns

That the ART's PI objectives are the right ones for the business; that a release can be
built, sold and serviced — not only demonstrated.

## Accountabilities

- Attend PI planning; assign business value to the ART's PI objectives.
- Attend the system demo; accept or challenge what is shown.
- Resolve escalations the [[Release Train Engineer]] brings.

## Decision Rights

- Decide PI content together with [[Product Management]] (**agree**).
- Decide to cut or defer scope mid-PI when the business case changes (autonomous as a
  group).

## Evaluation

### Metrics and Monitoring

- Business value achieved per PI objective; escalations resolved within the PI.
