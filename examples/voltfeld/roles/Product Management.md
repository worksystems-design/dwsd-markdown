## Purpose

### Driver

The [[Portfolio]] decides in epics, the teams work in stories; between them someone has to
hold one ranked list of features for the whole [[Charger ART]], or every team optimizes its
own.

### Requirement

A single role that owns the ART backlog — what the train builds next, across both product
lines and the platforms.

## Description

Product Management turns portfolio epics into features, ranks them for the ART, and defines
the content of each PI together with the [[Business Owner]]s. Kept by [[Niamh Byrne]], who
is also the Chief Product Officer — the role sits at FL2 while her title sits at FL3; the
two product owners, [[Product Owner Wallbox]] and [[Product Owner DC Charger]], split her
features into team backlogs.

## Concerns

The balance between the two product lines; platform work that no product line asks for but
both need; features that cannot ship until a certification or a tooling release lands.

## Accountabilities

- Own and rank the ART feature backlog; present the PI vision at PI planning.
- Accept features at the system demo.
- Keep the product roadmap and the ART backlog consistent with the [[Portfolio]].

## Decision Rights

- Ranks features and decides PI content — **agrees** with the [[Business Owner]]s.
- Decides feature acceptance (autonomous).
- Does not decide team-level story order — the product owners do.

## Evaluation

### Metrics and Monitoring

- Feature lead time; share of PI content delivered; features accepted at the system demo
  without rework.
