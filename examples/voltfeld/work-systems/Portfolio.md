## Purpose

Decide which chargers Voltfeld builds, for which markets, with what budget — and connect
that direction to the people who develop, build and sell them. The five C-level leaders
form this work system; its board is a portfolio kanban of epics. As FL3 it is the top of
the chain; nothing contributes upward from here.

## Key Responsibilities

- Decide the product roadmap across the [[Wallbox]] and [[DC Charger]] lines.
- Fund the [[Charger ART]] per PI and set its business priorities as [[Business Owner]]s.
- Set production volumes and the ramp plan with [[Manufacturing]].
- Own market entry, pricing and the major fleet contracts.

## Customers and Deliverables

- [[Charger ART]] — a prioritized set of epics and a PI budget.
- [[Manufacturing]] — volume targets and ramp dates.
- Investors and the board — margin and growth per product line.

## Dependencies

Depends on PI results and predictability from the [[Charger ART]], on build volumes and
yield from [[Manufacturing]], and on pipeline and field feedback from [[Sales]] and
[[Field Service]].

## Key Challenges

- Development and production report into the Portfolio through two different chains —
  the ART through [[Marit Solberg]] and [[Niamh Byrne]], the plant through
  [[Ingrid Halvorsen]] — and meet only here, at FL3.
- Hardware decisions are expensive to reverse; the portfolio has to decide earlier than
  the ART's cadence would suggest.

## Key Metrics and Monitoring

- Revenue and margin per product line; epic lead time; PI predictability of the ART;
  on-time production ramps.

## Evaluation Schedule
