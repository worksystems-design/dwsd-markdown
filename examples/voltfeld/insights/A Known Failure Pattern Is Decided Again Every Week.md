The [[Field Issue Triage]] spends its time on calls it has already made. A failure pattern
that is known, grouped correctly by the [[Field Issue Triage Agent]] and decided the same
way every time still waits for Tuesday and still takes a slot in the half hour. The
weekly decision adds nothing; what it costs is time for the issues that are new.

**Category:** flow | **Severity:** warning

## Observations

- Eleven of the month's triage calls were the same call ([[Same Failure Pattern Eleven Times in September]]).
- The agent's grouping matched the triage's decision every time; nobody changed it.
- Customers with the pattern waited up to six days for the workaround, because the answer
  came after Tuesday.
- The [[Expedite Lane Policy]] knows two outcomes, hotfix or feature, and no rule for a
  pattern that has already been decided.

## Suggested Actions

- Let a decided pattern skip the weekly call and go straight to the owning team.
- Keep the triage for what someone has to weigh.
