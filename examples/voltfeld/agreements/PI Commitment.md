## Driver and Requirement

A PI is a promise six teams make together. If features can enter mid-PI whenever someone
asks, the dependency board from PI planning is fiction within a month and the platform
teams are pulled in three directions. The train needs a rule for what may change inside
the ten weeks, and who may change it.

## Who is responsible for what?

- [[Product Management]] — brings new features to PI planning, not into a running PI.
- [[Business Owner]]s — the only ones who may add or cut a feature mid-PI, and only against
  a stated business reason.
- [[Release Train Engineer]] — makes every mid-PI change visible on the board and at the
  next [[ART Sync]].
- The teams — may re-sequence their own stories freely; may not take on a new feature
  without the above.

## Description

Features enter the `In PI` group of the [[Charger ART Board]] at PI planning. During the
PI, the group's content changes only by a [[Business Owner]] decision, recorded on the
card. Hotfixes are exempt: they travel the expedite lane under the
[[Expedite Lane Policy]], not this agreement.

## Evaluation Criteria

- PI predictability at or above 80% of committed objectives.
- Mid-PI feature changes per PI: visible, with a named Business Owner each; fewer than
  three.

## Concerns

Hardware does not always fit a ten-week box: a tooling slip is not a scope change anyone
decided, yet it moves a feature out of the PI. The rule covers decisions, not lead times.
