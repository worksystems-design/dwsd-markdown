The [[Charger ART]] needs tooling and ramp lead times inside its planning, or it will keep
committing to PI objectives it cannot hold.

## Current Situation

The train plans ten weeks at a time with the people who develop. Tooling (fourteen weeks)
and ramp (five weeks) are owned by [[Supply Chain]] and [[Manufacturing]], who are not in
the room and have no board position in the train. Two PIs in a row, an objective moved
because a mold arrived late.

## Effect

PI predictability has fallen to 70% in two PIs — evidenced by the
[[Inspect and Adapt]] measurements, not assumed. Customer units ship five weeks after
the release date the [[Business Owner]]s accepted at the [[System Demo]]. [[Sales]] has
started adding a buffer to every date it quotes.

## Need

The train needs to see and plan long-lead work one PI ahead, and the plant needs to hear
about a release before it is released. The [[Release Train Engineer]] has the need on the
train's side; the [[Manufacturing Lead]] on the plant's.

## Consequences

Objectives the train can actually hold; a ramp that starts at the demo, not at the release;
and the plant's producibility feedback arriving while the design can still change.
