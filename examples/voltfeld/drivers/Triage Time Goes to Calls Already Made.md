The [[Charger ART]] needs its half hour of triage for the issues nobody has decided yet,
and customers with a known fault need their answer without waiting for Tuesday.

## Current Situation

Every field issue goes through the weekly [[Field Issue Triage]], whether it is new or
the same pattern for the eleventh time. The [[Field Issue Triage Agent]] already groups the
known patterns correctly; the people confirm the grouping and repeat last week's call.

## Effect

About a third of the triage time in September went to repeat calls. New issues were pushed
to the end of the half hour or to the next week. A customer with a known fault waited up to
six days for an answer that was already written.

## Need

Repeat calls should not need the meeting, and the people in it should have the time for
what is new. The product owners ([[Lukas Weber]], [[Sofia Marques]]) have the need on the
train's side, [[Mateo Ruiz]] on the field's.

## Consequences

A shorter queue in front of the triage, faster answers to customers with a known fault, and
the triage's attention on the issues that need weighing.
