## Proposal

Two changes to how the [[Charger ART]] plans, from PI 27.1 on:

1. **Long-lead work is planned one PI ahead.** At PI planning *n*, [[HW Base]] names every
   mold and long-lead part the features of PI *n+1* will need; [[Supply Chain]] attends PI
   planning, takes the list, and the tooling dates go on the dependency board like any other
   dependency. Design freeze for a mold stays at the [[DFM Review]], not at planning.
2. **The plant joins the release.** The [[Manufacturing Lead]] becomes a participant of PI
   planning and the [[System Demo]], not a guest; fixture work starts when a feature is
   demoed, not when the release package arrives; and a ramp-readiness check at every
   System Demo lifts the plant's status back into the train.

Neither Supply Chain nor Manufacturing joins the ART as a team. They join its planning.

## Objections

- [[Wen Zhao]]: committing to tooling one PI ahead means ordering molds for designs that
  may still change — addressed by committing the *item and its date* at planning and the
  *geometry* at the DFM review, where it is frozen today anyway.
- [[Greta Vossberg]]: two more parties make PI planning longer — accepted; the long-lead
  board is prepared before the event, not built in it.
- [[Marit Solberg]] asked whether this is the moment to give production its own FL2 or to
  merge it into the train's board — deferred: run two PIs with the lighter change first.

## Outcome

Consented at the [[Portfolio Sync]] on 2026-10-06. Recorded as
[[Production Joins the Train's Planning]]; operationalized by
[[Long-Lead Commitment at PI Planning]] and [[Ramp Readiness Review]], both in force from
the PI 27.1 planning on 2026-12-03.
