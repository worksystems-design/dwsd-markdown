DC Power Stage builds the power cabinet of the DC chargers: cooling, cabling and the stacked [[HW Base]] power modules. Members: [[Ferdinand Achterberg]] and [[Mirela Popescu]].

A team, not a work system, with no board of its own. It belongs to the [[DC Charger]]
product line and contributes to it; the product line coordinates it at FL2, at PI planning
and the [[ART Sync]].
