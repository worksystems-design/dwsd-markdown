DC Power Stage builds the power cabinet of the DC chargers: cooling, cabling and the stacked [[HW Base]] power modules. Members: [[Ferdinand Achterberg]] and [[Mirela Popescu]].

A team, not a work system: it has no `flightlevel` and no board of its own. It belongs to
the [[DC Charger]] product line and contributes to it; the product line coordinates it at FL2.
The example draws no board for the DC Charger product line; the team plans with it at PI planning and the [[ART Sync]].
