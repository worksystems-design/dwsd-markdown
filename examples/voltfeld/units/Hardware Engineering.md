Hardware Engineering is a **line department**: the hardware, mechanical, test and
compliance engineers report to its head, [[Kaspar Reinholt]], who hires, develops and
reviews them. None of them *work* here — their `member-of` points at the agile team they
sit in ([[Wallbox]], [[DC Charger]], [[HW Base]], [[System Integration]]).

That is why this unit has a box in the units view but no members of its own: it is where
the reporting lines go, not where the work goes. Compare the org chart, where the same
people sit directly under Kaspar.
