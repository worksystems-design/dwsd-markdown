DC Site Controller builds the dispenser and site controller: high-power control firmware and the payment terminal integration. Members: [[Tomasz Wierzbicki]] and [[Jakob Brandt]].

A team, not a work system, with no board of its own. It belongs to the [[DC Charger]]
product line and contributes to it; the product line coordinates it at FL2, at PI planning
and the [[ART Sync]].
