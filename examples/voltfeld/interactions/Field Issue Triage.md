## Purpose

Thirty minutes every Tuesday: [[Mateo Ruiz]] brings the week's [[Field Issue]]s from
[[Field Service]], the two product owners ([[Lukas Weber]], [[Sofia Marques]]) and
[[Ravi Anand]] from [[Connectivity]] sit with him, and the group starts from the list the
[[Field Issue Triage Agent]] has clustered and ranked overnight. For each issue one call:
**hotfix** — safety or a fleet down, into the expedite lane now — or **feature**, into the
program backlog for the next PI planning. That call is the fork in the
[[Field Issue Route]], and this is where it is made.

Anchored three ways: on the `Triage` column of the expedite lane, on the route's
`issue @ Train` stage, and on [[Field Service]] itself — a plain team with no board, so the
marker sits on its box in the units view. Field Service is not in the ART; this half hour
is its door into the train.
