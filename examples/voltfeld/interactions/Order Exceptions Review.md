## Purpose

One hour on the first Wednesday of the month: [[Paula Weiss]], [[Hanna Berglund]],
[[Leyla Demir]], [[Wen Zhao]] and the ERP partner's consultant. The group starts from the
month's reason codes, which the [[Order Exception Agent]] has grouped. The question is not
"which cases were hard" but "**why do orders fall out of the factory**" — and which of
those reasons is worth a change: an ERP rule, a new check for the agent, an agreement.

This is the improvement loop that used to be a ticket queue. A pattern that keeps coming
back becomes a signal, and from there it climbs the ladder like any other observation — see
[[Configuration Cases Pile Up in the Studio]].

## What is the outcome?

At most two changes a month, each with an owner and a date; the rest stays on the list.
